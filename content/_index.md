Welcome to the home of the Furries of RIT, locally known as FurRIT. We are the
group for Furries who attend, work at, or are otherwise affiliated with the
Rochester Institute of Technology in Rochester, NY, USA.

Connect with other furries in your major, the same year as you, and share the
same interests. Link to and share social media, art galleries, and game friend
codes. Find out about and join in on meet ups, gatherings, and events of all
sorts.

If you are interested in joining our online and in-person community, please
follow the link below! Whether you're an incoming freshman, longtime alumni, or
somewhere in between, a staff or faculty member, all Furry members of the RIT
Community are welcome, here.
