This is the home of the Furries of RIT, known as FurRIT. We are a group of
Furries who attend, work at, or are otherwise affiliated with the Rochester
Institute of Technology in Rochester, NY, USA.

Use this site to connect with other furries in your major, the same year as
you, or share the same interests. Link to and share social media, art galleries,
and game friend codes. Find out about and join in on meet ups, gatherings, and
events of all sorts.
